This policy explains what happens to your files and data when you use Nexora Tools (tools.codywork.com), a product operated by Codywork. The short version: **tools marked "On your device" never upload your files**, we do not have user accounts, and we never send file names or file contents to analytics.

## Your files

Every tool shows where it runs. All tools currently available are marked **On your device**. For these tools:

- Your files are read and processed by your own browser, in the memory of the tab you are using.
- Files are **not uploaded** to Codywork or to any third party. We cannot see, store or recover them.
- Results exist only in that browser tab until you download them, start over or close the page. We do not save copies of your files or results on your device.

If we add a tool that needs server processing in the future, it will be clearly labeled before you use it, and this policy will be updated first to explain what is uploaded, why, where it is processed and how quickly it is deleted.

## What is stored on your device

Nexora Tools does not set its own cookies. We use your browser's local storage only for preferences:

| Key | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Language | Remembers the language you chose. |
| Theme | Remembers dark or light mode. |
| Recently used tools | A list of tool identifiers (never file names) shown on the homepage. |
| Language suggestion | Remembers that you dismissed the language banner. |
| Analytics choice | Remembers whether you allowed analytics (only if analytics is offered). |

You can clear these at any time from your browser settings.

## Analytics

We may use Google Analytics 4 to understand which tools are used and where they fail, so we can improve them. Analytics is **only loaded after you give consent** in the banner; if you decline, no analytics script is requested. When enabled, events describe product behavior, for example "the Merge PDF tool processed 3 files successfully". They **never include file names, file contents, text you type or other document information**. Google signals and ad personalization are disabled.

## Hosting and security

The website is delivered by Cloudflare, which acts as our hosting and content delivery provider. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes standard technical request data, such as IP address, browser user agent and request time, to deliver the site and protect it against abuse. This data is not linked to your files, which never reach our servers.

## Third-party services

- **Cloudflare:** hosting, content delivery and security.
- **Google Analytics:** usage statistics, only with your consent.

All code needed to process files, including open-source libraries, is served from our own domain.

## Children

Nexora Tools is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your rights

Because we do not have accounts and do not receive your files, we hold very little information that could relate to you. Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. To exercise these rights or ask any privacy question, contact us through the [contact page](/en/contact/).

## Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and adjust the date above. Significant changes, such as introducing a server-based tool, will be published here before they take effect.
