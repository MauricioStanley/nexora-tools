Nexora Tools is a growing collection of fast, focused online tools for everyday file tasks: merging and splitting PDFs, compressing and converting images, creating QR codes and more. Every tool is free to use and none of them require an account.

## How it works

Most tools run entirely inside your web browser. When a tool is marked **On your device**, your files are read, processed and turned into a download by your browser itself. They are never uploaded to a server. That makes these tools private by design, and usually faster too, because there is no upload or download queue.

Some future tools, such as advanced text recognition or heavy document conversion, may need server processing to work well. Those tools will say so clearly before you use them, and our [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy/) will describe exactly what happens to your files.

## What we care about

- **Real tools, not tricks.** A tool is only published when it actually works. We explain its limitations instead of hiding them.
- **Privacy.** We never ask for more than a task needs, and we never send file names or file contents to analytics.
- **Speed and accessibility.** Pages are lightweight, work on phones first and follow accessibility best practices.
- **No friction.** No sign-up walls, no watermarks, no surprise email capture.
