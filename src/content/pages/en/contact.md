We'd love to hear from you, whether you found a bug, have a question about privacy, or want to suggest a tool.

## Reporting a problem

To help us fix issues quickly, please include:

- the tool you were using and the page address;
- your device, browser and browser version (for example "iPhone 15, Safari 18" or "Windows 11, Chrome 128");
- what you expected to happen and what happened instead;
- the approximate file size and type, if relevant.

**Please don't send us confidential files.** If a file triggers a problem, describe it instead, or share a version that contains no personal information.

## Suggesting a tool

Tell us what you were trying to do and which file types were involved. Suggestions shape what we build next.

## Privacy questions

For any question about how Nexora Tools handles data, including requests related to your rights, write to the email address above. See also our [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy/).
